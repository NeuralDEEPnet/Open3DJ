            Shortcuts work while a slider or a checkbox has focus, and with Caps Lock on.

            Mouse presets

            Open3DJLeft drag orbits, right drag pans, wheel zooms at the cursor
            SolidWorksMiddle drag orbits, Ctrl+middle drag pans, wheel zooms at the cursor
            OnshapeRight drag orbits, middle drag (or Ctrl+right drag) pans, wheel zooms at the cursor

            A left click selects the part and the face or edge under the cursor in every preset; Shift+click or Ctrl+click adds to the selection. On a part that is small on screen a click keeps the face; zoom in to pick its edges. Double-click a tree row to fit that part.

            With "Share with agents" on, what you select (parts, and faces as element addresses) is saved in the project, where an agent reads it with inspect fields=[selection]. An agent can point at parts and faces with render view=highlight; they show in magenta with a note, without moving your view, until you dismiss them.

            Labels: measured numbers come from the CPU kernel (the report); observed numbers are read off the display mesh; preview overlays show where to look, and the kernel decides.
